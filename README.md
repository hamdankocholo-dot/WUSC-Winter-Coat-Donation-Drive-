# WUSC McGill Winter Coat Donation Drive

## Motivation

This project was inspired by the need for a centralized platform for WUSC McGill donation initiatives. Because student executive positions change regularly, donors may not always know who to contact regarding donations. This website aims to provide a consistent source of information, donation guidelines, and communication for community members who wish to support refugee students.

---

## Project Overview

The WUSC McGill Winter Coat Donation Drive website is a multi-page informational platform designed to support donation initiatives organized by the WUSC McGill Local Committee.

The website provides information about WUSC, the Student Refugee Program (SRP), accepted donation items, and ways for donors to contribute to initiatives such as the Winter Coat Project.

The platform serves as a permanent point of reference for students, faculty, alumni, and community members interested in supporting refugee students settling into life at McGill and in Montreal.

---

## Problem Statement

WUSC McGill receives donations throughout the academic year, particularly through initiatives such as the Winter Coat Project.

However:

- Student leadership positions change regularly.
- Donors may not know who currently coordinates donations.
- Information about accepted items may not be easily accessible.
- Communication often depends on individual contacts.

This project provides a centralized website that helps address these challenges by offering consistent information and contact pathways.

---

## Objectives

The goals of this project are to:

- Promote WUSC McGill donation initiatives.
- Increase awareness of the Student Refugee Program.
- Provide clear donation guidelines.
- Help donors identify accepted items.
- Connect donors with the appropriate WUSC representatives.
- Improve continuity between changing executive teams.

## Website Features

### Home Page

- Introduction to the Winter Coat Donation Drive
- Website mission statement
- Donation call-to-action
- Direct access to the donation form

### About WUSC-McGill

Provides information about:

- WUSC
- The Student Refugee Program (SRP)
- WUSC at McGill
- Other WUSC McGill initiatives

### Donation Guidelines

Provides information regarding:

#### Accepted Items

- Winter clothing
- School supplies
- Everyday clothing
- Household items
- Bedding
- Electronics

#### Non-Accepted Items

- Damaged items
- Large furniture
- Expired food
- Hazardous materials

### Donation Form

Allows donors to submit:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Donation description
- Condition of items
- Preferred delivery method
- Additional notes

### Contact Page

Provides contact information for the VP Librarian and Office Manager responsible for coordinating donation-related activities and the Winter Coat Project.
