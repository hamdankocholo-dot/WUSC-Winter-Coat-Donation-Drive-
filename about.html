What is WUSC?

The World University Service of Canada (WUSC) is a Canadian non-profit organization founded in the 1920s. Working in over 30 countries, WUSC creates opportunities for young people through education, economic development and community engagement.

A key part of WUSC's mission is supporting refugees and displaced youth by helping them access higher education and build a better future for themselves and their communities through Student Refugee Program (SRP).

The Student Refugee Program (SRP)

The SRP helps refugee students resettle in Canada while pursuing post-secondary education. Through partnerships with Canadian universities and colleges, students receive support as they begin a new chapter in their academic and personal lives.

WUSC McGill

At McGill University, the SRP is managed and supported by the WUSC McGill Local Committee, a student-run SSMU club and it provides day-to-day social and academic support for the SRP scholars besides raising funds, and awareness for the program and refugee education issues on campus.

The WUSC McGill Local Committee was formed in 1986 and has sponsored close to 91 scholars as of 2025. The SRP provides a year of full financial sponsorship, integration assistance, and a community of support. This is funded through a $4 levy collected from all McGill students every semester based on student referendum, which is administered by Scholarships and Student Aid.

At present, the fund can support an average of 9 new refugee students each year. In the 2026-2027 academic year, 10 students were sponsored.

Other projects run by WUSC McGill

Along with supporting SRP Scholars as they navigate McGill, Montreal, and Canada, and raising awareness about refugee issues on campus, WUSC McGill is responsible for several student-led initiatives.

The WUSC Library
The WUSC Library is a wonderful resource available to all students who wish to loan a textbook for the duration of their course. This project was created to help mitigate the rising cost of textbooks.
The WUSC Winter Coat project
The WUSC Winter Coat project is another initiative available for all students on campus. We keep a regular stock of winter coats to help students during their first winter in Canada. We also accept donations of winter coats from students who no longer need theirs. Although priority is given to the SRP scholars, any student at McGill is welcome to contact us regarding loaning books or coats.
The McGill Journal of Refugee and Migration studies (MJRMS)
The journal's main objective is to support the WUSC McGill local committee in its goal of spreading awareness about refugee issues and rights. To this end, the Journal publishes works pertaining to refugee and migration issues, written by undergraduate and graduate students from McGill, in both English and French.

Why This Donation Platform?

This website was created to help community members, students, faculty, and alumni connect with WUSC McGill's donation initiatives through The WUSC Winter Coat Project. Donations help newly arrived scholars access essential clothing, household items, school supplies, and winter necessities as they settle into life in Montreal.

Every donation, whether a winter coat, textbook, or household item, helps support scholars during their transition to studying and living in Canada.
