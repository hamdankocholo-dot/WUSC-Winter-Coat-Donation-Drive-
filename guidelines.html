We accept the following items:

We are glad to receive the following items:

Winter Clothing

  • Winter jackets/parkas
  • Winter boots
  • Gloves
  • Scarves
  • Hats/tuques
  • Sweaters/hoodies

School Supplies

  • Backpacks
  • Notebooks
  • Binders
  • Calculators
  • Textbooks for introductory university courses

Everyday Clothing

  • T-shirts
  • Shirts
  • Pants
  • Jeans
  • Light jackets
  • Casual shoes

Household Items

  • Pots
  • Pans
  • Plates
  • Bowls
  • Cups
  • Mugs
  • Cutlery
  • Food storage containers

Bedding

  • Blankets
  • Comforters
  • Bedsheets
  • Pillowcases

Electronics

  • Monitors
  • Keyboards
  • Mice
  • Computer Accessories
  • Laptops

Items Not Accepted:

Sorry, we are currently not accepting the following items:

Damaged Items

  • Torn clothing
  • Broken appliances
  • Missing Parts
  • Heavily stained items

Large Furniture

  • Couches
  • Large wardrobes
  • Large dining tables

Expired Foods

  • Expired canned food
  • Open food products
  • Perishable food items

Hazardous Materials

  • Paints
  • Chemicals
  • Propane tanks
  • Unlabeled cleaning products

Note:

Submit the donation form or contact the VP Librarian and Office Manager.
We will be happy to assist you.

All donated items should be clean, safe and in good usable conditions.
